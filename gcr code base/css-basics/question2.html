History of Agra

Agra is a historic city in Uttar Pradesh known for its beautiful architecture and rich cultural heritage. The city has played an important role in Indian history.

Famous Places

Agra is famous for the Taj Mahal, Agra Fort and other historical monuments. These places attract visitors from different parts of the world.

Culture of Agra

Agra has a rich mixture of traditional art, architecture, festivals and local traditions. Its historical background can be seen in its buildings and daily life.

Famous Food

Agra is also known for its local food and sweets. Traditional dishes and famous sweets are an important part of the city's food culture.
